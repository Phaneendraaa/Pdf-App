Pdf Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Pdf Kit is a PDF toolkit that works on your phone. In short: we do not collect, store or share any of your data. Your files never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Your files

Every tool (scan, convert, compress, edit, sign, redact, OCR and the rest) runs on your phone. Files you create or import are stored in the app's private folder on your device. We have no servers and never receive your documents.

Information we collect

None. Pdf Kit has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only settings it keeps (like theme, autofill details you type in, or your saved signature) stay on your phone in the app's private storage and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Camera and photos

The scanner uses Google's on-device document scanner. Photos you pick are read only to make the PDF or image you asked for. Pdf Kit does not upload them.

Google Play services

Text recognition, the document scanner and on-device summaries use Google Play services components that run on your phone. Google Play services may download these models the first time you use a feature; that download is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. Your documents are not sent with it.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Save or Open with, the file goes only to the app or place you pick. If you send feedback or a crash report, you see and send it yourself through your own email or chat app; it contains the app version, Android version, phone model and, for crashes, technical error details. No document content is included.

Children

Pdf Kit is a general productivity app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
